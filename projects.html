Overview

An end-to-end recruitment operations platform designed for high-volume UK healthcare agencies. ShiftRadius bridges dynamic daily candidate availability sheets with real-time incoming shift demand, enforces true road-network commute limits, and automates multi-provider compliance paperwork.

90% less compliance admin
< 30 sec shift dispatch, down from 15+ minutes
Fewer travel-related cancellations

Key highlights & features

  • Smart hybrid commute engine: replaced misleading straight-line distances with true road-network driving time and mileage through an asynchronous OSRM road matrix pipeline.
  • Mutual match & 1-click outreach: matches candidates by Day/Night shift patterns (D, N, D/N) and commute thresholds (up to 60–90 mins), with instant pre-formatted WhatsApp and SMS links.
  • Compliance document cross-population: an XML-level Word (.docx) extraction and generation engine that maps candidate data across 19+ care provider proformas (Care UK, NAS, Salutem, Mencap, Leonard Cheshire and more), eliminating redundant paperwork.
  • Role hierarchy & safety guards: strict healthcare role rules (for example, HCAs can cover Kitchen Assistant shifts but not vice versa, and Senior Care roles require Medication Competency verification).
  • Geospatial demand intelligence: an interactive Leaflet map showing candidate density clusters against underserved care homes (fewer than 3 available workers within 45 mins) and 8-day forward shift demand.

Architecture & technical challenges

  • API rate limits at scale (15,000+ pairs): chunked requests into batches of 90, added exponential backoff retries, and built a multi-tier persistent database and session cache.
  • Render storms and UI lag: converted rapid background state emissions into a 1.5-second throttled debounce and deferred heavy 30,000-matrix calculations until modal interaction, keeping the UI at 60 FPS.
  • Document table and date parsing: re-engineered the .docx XML parser with date-first regex patterns, so UK-standard DD-MM-YYYY completion dates take priority over simple Yes/No values in complex tables.
  • 2-tier geocoding fallback: automatic fallback from exact UK postcodes to town centroids, with visible accuracy badges (Exact vs Approx).
